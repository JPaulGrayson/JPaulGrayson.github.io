About Paul

I've been building software since 1970. In 1983 I cofounded Micrografx (NASDAQ: MGXI), where we created PC-Draw, the first drawing program for the PC, and In*a*Vision, the first application for Microsoft Windows, and built parts of IBM's OS/2. Micrografx went public in 1990 and was acquired by Corel. I cofounded Axxis Software, whose TAXI was one of the first mobile mapping and navigation products (sold to News Corp), and founded Alibre, a 3D CAD company I led until 3D Systems acquired it in 2012.

Today I run Rough Creek Ranch, a horse rescue and wildlife refuge in Clayton, Oklahoma, and I’m building again, this time with AI helpers. These apps are the result. Tell me what you think.
